Records from 1993 on are TOP500 Linpack Rmax (FP64), through the June 2026 list. Earlier figures are historical estimates, and the machines before 1954 counted operations rather than floating-point operations.

Device speeds are rough estimates. FP64 is sustained double-precision throughput, close to what Linpack would measure. FP32 is the GPU's single-precision spec-sheet peak, compared against the same FP64 records, which flatters modern hardware a lot.
